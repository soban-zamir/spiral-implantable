# Compact Multiband Spiral Shaped Implantable Antenna

Replication of a compact multiband spiral shaped implantable antenna for scalp implantation and leadless pacemaker systems, covering the MICS (402 to 405 MHz), ISM (433.1 to 434.8 MHz and 2400 to 2483.5 MHz), and midfield (1520 to 1693 MHz) bands. The design uses a two arm symmetrical spiral radiator on a high dielectric Rogers RT/duroid 6010 substrate and superstrate, with an open end hook shaped slot in the ground for miniaturization and bandwidth enhancement, giving a total volume of 17.15 mm3 (7 mm x 6.5 mm x 0.377 mm). The model was rebuilt and simulated in CST Microwave Studio, and the design files are provided in this repository.
